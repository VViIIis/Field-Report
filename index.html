Field Report

Upload your contest standings and see how your lineups stacked up against the whole field: finishes, ownership, stacks, projections and money, contest by contest.

Your files are read on this device and never uploaded. They're remembered in this browser until you clear them.

What to upload

  1. Contest standings (required). On DraftKings, open a completed contest and use Export Lineups to CSV (or the standings download). One file per contest; add as many as you like.
  2. Entry history (optional). My Contests → History → Download Entry History. Adds entry fees, winnings and dates for automatic P&L.
  3. Salaries or projections (optional). The slate's DKSalaries.csv or a projections export with player, team and salary columns. Unlocks stacks, and projection rank if it has projections.
