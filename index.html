Upload your contest standings and see how your lineups stacked up against the whole field: finishes, ownership, stacks, projections and money, contest by contest.

Your files are read on this device and never uploaded. They're remembered in this browser until you clear them.

Where to get your files on DraftKings

  1. Contest standings required
    On a computer, go to My Contests → History and open a finished contest. Under the standings, click Export Lineups to CSV. Repeat for each contest you want in the report.
  2. Entry history optional
    My Contests → History → Download Entry History. Adds fees, winnings, dates and week numbers, and finds your username automatically.
  3. Salaries or projections optional
    The slate's DKSalaries.csv, or any projections export with player, team and salary columns. Turns on the stack and projection charts.
